✿ About these letters

Arabic is read and written from right to left. Each letter has its own shape and sound. In the next 28 pages, we will meet every letter, learn a word that begins with it, and practise writing it. Turn the page to start with أ Alif!

A Word for Young Learners
Dear young learner,

You have reached the final pages of your first Arabic course book. You met all 28 letters of the Arabic alphabet, learnt many new words, practised writing, and played with pictures.

Arabic is the language of the Qur'an and the language of millions of people around the world. Every word you learn is a small treasure — and you already have many!

Keep practising a little every day. Say a new Arabic word when you wake up, when you eat your breakfast, when you see the sun or the moon. Soon, Arabic will feel like an old friend.

With love and prayers,
