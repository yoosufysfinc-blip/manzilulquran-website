DUAS FOR EVERY DAY
Tharbiya · Level 2 · Ages 9–12

A word for every moment of your day

Duʿā is not only for when something goes wrong. There is a word for waking, for eating, for leaving the house, for being afraid. Learn one each week until your whole day is wrapped in them.

Start with waking up →

How each online lesson runs

  1. Before class — read the Opening Question and look at the picture plates.
  2. In class — your teacher recites each duʿā and you repeat. Then you work through the seven memorising steps together, and answer the Talk About It questions in the chat or unmuted.
  3. After class — finish the memorising steps at home and do This Week's Task.
  4. Next class — recite your duʿā with your camera on, from memory, without reading.
