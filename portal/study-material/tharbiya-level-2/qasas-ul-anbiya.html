Ten lives that lit the world

Allah sent messengers to every people. Ten of their stories are here — each one a real life, with real fear, real courage and real trust in Allah.

How each online lesson runs

  1. Before class — read the Opening Question and look at the three picture plates on your own.
  2. In class — your teacher shares the screen and reads the three scenes with you, then opens the Talk About It questions. Answer in the chat or unmute.
  3. After class — do This Week's Task at home and send it to your teacher before the next lesson.
