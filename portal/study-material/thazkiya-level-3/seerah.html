THE LIFE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Thazkiya · Level 3 · Ages 11–15

The one life worth knowing by heart

From an orphan in Makkah to the mercy sent for all the worlds — and he is still ahead of us. Fifteen lessons, each one written so that by the end you do not only know him. You love him.

Begin →

How each online lesson runs

  1. Before class — read the Opening Question and look at the two picture plates.
  2. In class — your teacher reads the three scenes with you, then What He Was Like, then opens the discussion. Answer in the chat or unmute.
  3. After class — do This Week's Task and send it to your teacher.

And we are not finished

You have read his whole life — and he is still waiting for us at the Ḥawḍ. Send ṣalawāt on him today. He hears it, and he answers.
