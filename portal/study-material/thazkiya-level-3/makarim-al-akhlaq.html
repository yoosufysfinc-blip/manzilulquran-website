The second book of character

Noorul Hayah taught the habits you can see — telling the truth, helping, keeping clean. This book is about the ones nobody can see: why you did it, who you did it for, and what you do when it costs you something.

How each online lesson runs

  1. Before class — read the opening question and look at the two plates.
  2. In class — your teacher reads the explanation and the two stories with you, then opens the discussion. Answer in the chat or unmute.
  3. Afterwards — the reminder at the end of the lesson is the part to carry with you.

Mā shā' Allah

Twelve traits, and not one of them can be seen from outside. That is the point. Allah looks at the heart, and the heart is the only thing you truly control.
